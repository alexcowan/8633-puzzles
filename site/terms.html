Terms, Terms, Terms

There are a lot of new words in this course. You don't need all of them. What you do need, for each one, is where it fits: which part of the app it belongs to, which of the four steps it comes up in, which engineering idea it bears on, and how people use it (and misuse it). Take a term, quiz yourself, then see what I'd say. Most terms have more than one sensible answer; I'll show you mine (solid ring) and the ones I'd also call reasonable (dashed ring). Nothing here is scored or recorded.

What the choices mean

The pipeline. Everything a team does to go from an idea to a working user experience and back again, in four areas: continuous design, application development, continuous delivery, and hypothesis testing. Some terms are about this machinery around the app rather than the app itself.

MVC. The app itself, in three parts. The Model is the data the app keeps. The View is what a user, or another machine, sees and uses. The Controller is the code that makes things happen between them. Some terms span all three.

The four steps. How any one piece gets built, mostly inside application development. 1. Focusing and communicating design intent. 2. Unpacking what you want to have happen into codable steps. 3. Effectuating between alternatives. 4. Testing and debugging. Some terms don't belong to any one step.
