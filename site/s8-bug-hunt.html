Bug report · HINH-482 · Severity: high

Same-day parts show "Ships in 3 days"

Reported by
Dispatch, after two technicians asked why nothing is ever available today.
Why it matters
Technicians are telling customers to wait, or ordering a pricier part that says it ships sooner, when the right part is sitting at a branch today.
Scenario
GIVEN Huey has HX-2201 in stock at a branch that can hand it over today (a lead time of 0 days)
WHEN Trent searches for HX-2201
THEN Huey's offer shows "Available today"
What happens
Huey's offer for HX-2201 shows "Ships in 3 days." Lewy's offer for CP-0450, which Lewy says is ready today, shows the same thing.
What's fine
Parts with a lead time of 2 days show "Ships in 2 days." Parts a distributor doesn't have show "Not available."
To reproduce
Search for HX-2201. Look at Huey's row.
