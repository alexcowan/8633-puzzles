Never available today

The story: As Trent the Technician, I want to find a part's price and availability so I can decide on next steps with the customer.

To answer, the lookup asks two parts distributors, Huey and Lewy. For each part, a distributor reports a price, whether it's in stock, and a lead time: how many days until the technician can have it. A lead time of 0 means today.

A bug report just came in about HinH's parts lookup. Your job is to find the bug the analytical way: say what you expect before each check, look, and let each look rule out part of the path. Hints are here if you get stuck, one at a time. Nothing is scored until you ask, and nothing is recorded.

Bug report · HINH-482 · Severity: high

Same-day parts show "Ships in 3 days"

Reported by
Dispatch, after two technicians asked why nothing is ever available today.
Why it matters
Technicians are telling customers to wait, or ordering a pricier part that says it ships sooner, when the right part is available today.
Scenario
GIVEN Huey has HX-2201 in stock with a lead time of 0 days
WHEN Trent searches for HX-2201
THEN Huey's offer shows "Available today"
What happens
Huey's offer for HX-2201 shows "Ships in 3 days." Lewy's offer for CP-0450, also a lead time of 0, shows the same thing.
What's fine
Parts with a lead time of 2 days show "Ships in 2 days." Parts a distributor doesn't have show "Not available."
To reproduce
Search for HX-2201. Look at Huey's row.

The code

Unpacking the code, it basically executes these five steps:

  1. The View gets the part number and asks the controller to find it (searchParts).
  2. The controller asks each distributor about the part (askHuey, askLewy).
  3. Each distributor's answer goes through its adapter to normalize the data (normalizeHuey, normalizeLewy).
  4. The controller labels each offer with its availability (describeAvailability).
  5. The controller sends the offers back to the View.

The controller comes first and runs the steps in order. Below it are the functions it calls in steps 3 and 4. The distributors' systems (step 2) are outside HinH's code, so they aren't shown; look A shows what they send.
