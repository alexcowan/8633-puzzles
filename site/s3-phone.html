Breaks at the phone

Trent uses HinH on his phone, standing in a customer's basement. The story: As Trent the Technician, I want to see a part's price and whether it's in stock, so I can quote the customer on the spot.

On a laptop the page below looks fine. Your job is to find out what Trent sees on his phone, and why, using your browser's developer tools. Don't read the source first.

At HinH, parts.css is a component of their design system: any parts-related page uses it, it changes a few times a year, and the pages change nearly every week. So the pages depend on the design system, never the other way around.

Be ready to explain
  1. What the story says Trent should see on his phone, and what he actually gets.
  2. Which element is too wide, and by how much, using the four layers of the box model.
  3. The one-line change you'd commit (file and line), and why it works on every phone, not only the one you tested.

1. Predict first

Before you open any tools.

Given
Trent's phone screen is 390 pixels wide,
When
he searches for CP,
Then
Opening the developer tools, and pretending to be a phone

Use Chrome or another Chromium browser (Edge, Brave, Arc). Right-click anything in the HinH app and choose Inspect, or press Cmd+Option+I on a Mac (Ctrl+Shift+I on Windows).

Device toolbar. At the top left of the tools there's a small phone-and-tablet icon. Click it (or press Cmd+Shift+M, Ctrl+Shift+M on Windows, while the tools have focus). The page redraws at a phone's size. From the menu above the page, pick iPhone 12 Pro, or type 390 in the width box.

Box model. Select an element in the Elements panel, then open the Computed tab. The nested boxes at the top are that element's margin, border, padding, and content, with the size of each.

You can change any value in the Styles pane, or untick it. That changes this tab only. Nothing is saved.

Safari: turn on Settings → Advanced → “Show features for web developers,” then Develop → Enter Responsive Design Mode. Ad blockers and password managers sometimes add their own errors and elements to the tools. That's normal; ignore anything that isn't about this page.

2. Run it on a phone

Open the tools, turn on the device toolbar at 390 px, and search for CP in the app.

3. Find it, fix it in the tools, then check

  1. Find the element that's wider than the space it has. The box model in the Computed tab tells you by how much.
  2. Find the rule in Styles that makes it that wide, and change it right there in the tools.
  3. Click Check the page. It takes the styles as they are in your tab right now and tries them on a 390 px phone and a 1,200 px laptop, whatever size your window is.
