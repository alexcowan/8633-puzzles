Where the fix goes

The story: As Trent the Technician, I want to see a part's price and whether I can get it today, so I can agree on next steps with the customer.

The puzzle has two parts:

  1. Choose. Price and availability can come from three places: HinH's own copy of the catalog, the distributor's live system, or a language model. You try each one, change one line to pick, and decide which you'd ship.
  2. Fix. Then the distributor updates its system, and the page breaks. You decide where the fix goes.

The story's implementation is ten lines of showParts, and you never touch it. Everything you change is below a dashed line in the code: the interface between the story's implementation and wherever price and availability come from.

There's no library on the menu. A library is someone else's code running in your app; nobody publishes a package that knows what's on the distributor's shelf this morning.

Be ready to explain
  1. Which version you'd ship for Trent's story and why, in terms of HinH's business, and what it depends on that changes on its own schedule that HinH doesn't control.
  2. What happened when you asked the model for the same part twice, and what that does to a Given-When-Then for this story.
  3. The fix you'd commit when the distributor ships v2, and why the number of lines changed in showParts is the test.
